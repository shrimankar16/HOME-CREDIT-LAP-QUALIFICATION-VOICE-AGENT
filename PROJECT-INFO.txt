===============================================================================
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
===============================================================================

AI Model: Claude 3.5 Sonnet (recommended) or GPT-4
Voice: Retell-Cimo with Eleven Labs Flash v2
Language: English (en-US)
Vocab Specialization: Finance
Response Mode: Streaming (low latency)
Interruption Handling: Dynamic with backchannel
Call Duration: Up to 15 minutes
Silence Timeout: 5 minutes

===============================================================================
DATA COLLECTED
===============================================================================

1. Property Type (Residential/Commercial)
2. Property Ownership (Self-owned/Co-owned)
3. Original Documents Availability (Yes/No) - DISQUALIFIER
4. Requested Loan Amount (Up to ₹75 lakh)
5. Occupation (Salaried/Self-employed/Business owner)
6. Income Receipt Method (Bank/Cash/Cheque/Mixed)
7. Approximate Market Value
8. Preferred Repayment Tenure (1-15 years)

===============================================================================
COMPLIANCE & SAFETY
===============================================================================

The agent is designed for PRELIMINARY QUALIFICATION ONLY.

NEVER does:
❌ Promise final loan approval
❌ Quote specific interest rates
❌ Guarantee disbursement amounts or timelines
❌ Make assumptions about unclear answers

ALWAYS does:
✅ Set clear expectations
✅ Hand off to human experts for final decisions
✅ Clarify ambiguous responses
✅ Provide excellent customer experience
